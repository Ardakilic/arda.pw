I never wrote proper tasting notes for this one, so this is a short post.

## Verdict

A few months later, my take was simple: Monkey Shoulder (both the original and the [Smokey Monkey](/posts/monkey-shoulder-smokey-monkey-tasting-notes/)), along with Teacher's Highland Cream and Glenlivet Founder's Reserve, are whiskies you can sip every day without burning a hole in your pocket.

If you want a bit of smoke, though, go for the Smokey Monkey.
