Going Irish tonight. This was my first time trying this whisky: Bushmills #3 Char Bourbon Cask, a triple-distilled single malt.

A friend picked it up for me at a duty free with only 4 minutes to spare. I saw "char" on the label and, with plain logic, thought *it must be smoky, get that one*. It was also about 15 € cheaper than Talisker 10, and I figured I might as well try an Irish one for once.

These are my first impressions after a small taste.

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Whisky** | Bushmills #3 Char Bourbon Cask Reserve (The Steamship Collection) |
| **Type** | Single Malt Irish Whiskey, triple distilled |
| **Region** | Northern Ireland |
| **ABV** | 40% |
| **Size** | 1 L |
| **Price paid** | ~15 € cheaper than Talisker 10 at duty free |

## Tasting Notes

**Nose:** The only weak spot for me. If I had judged it by the nose alone, it wouldn't have impressed me this much.

**Palate:** As someone who loves smoky whisky, I definitely expected smoke at first, especially with "bourbon cask" and "charred" on the label. It's not that smoky, but it's a very aromatic whisky. I got vanilla, fresh citrus, even a slightly unripe, a bit sour orange.

**Finish:** Going down the throat it gets a bit harsher, and I caught myself saying *"oh, cinnamon, and I think there's honey in this too"*.

## Pairing

I opened some salted peanuts alongside it :D

## Verdict

I liked it quite a lot. It's not what I usually go for, since I'd love to always find Talisker Dark Storm or Lagavulin 16, but that doesn't always happen, so I try different flavours like this one.

This bottle also made me trust the brand. Later on I picked up their [Black Bush PX Sherry Cask](/posts/bushmills-black-bush-px-sherry-cask-tasting-notes/) partly because this one was so decent.

Sláinte! 🥃
