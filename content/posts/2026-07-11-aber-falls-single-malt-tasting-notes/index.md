I was looking for a cheap, light summer whisky and spotted this at Carrefour. It was 1700 TL for 70 cl. I quickly read some reviews: they said it flows lightly, but don't expect much complexity. I wasn't expecting complexity anyway, so it could be a good-value summer bottle.

Aber Falls Single Malt, from Wales.

I opened it the same night.

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Whisky** | Aber Falls Single Malt |

| **Country** | Wales |

| **Size** | 70 cl |
| **Price paid** | 1700 TL (Carrefour, July 2026) |

## Tasting Notes

**Nose:** Light and nice. No smoke, as expected, but I caught a very faint, cinnamon-like smell.

**Palate:** Harsher than I expected, but you get used to it after 2–3 sips. Very fruity, and it went down really clean: lemon, grapefruit and vanilla.

**Finish:** Towards the end, the aftertaste gives way to something like black pepper and dark chocolate.

## Verdict

Would I recommend it? I don't know. At 1700 TL, I could say yes. I bought it with low expectations anyway, as a cheap single malt I'd never tried, just to see what it's like.

A month later, Carrefour had it down to 1200 TL during a sale. At that price it's an easier yes.

Sláinte! 🥃
