I opened my second Cyprus spoil today: Dalwhinnie 15.

At first sip, the taste felt a bit unusual compared to everything I'd tried so far 😅 so I tasted it some more before trying to put it into words.

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Whisky** | Dalwhinnie 15 Year Old |
| **Type** | Single Malt Scotch Whisky |
| **Region** | Highlands |
| **ABV** | 43% |
| **Size** | 1 L |
| **Price paid** | 55 € (Cyprus) |

## Tasting Notes

**Nose:** Soft as anything, fruity and vanilla-y. A light smoke and peat say "I'm here too", but it's all balanced. A very pleasant nose.

**Palate:** Unexpectedly fruit-heavy, but in a fermented, tangy kind of way. There's an almost bitter fruit(?) taste that goes towards sharp vinegar. Next to these fruity flavours, there's also something soft, like honey. Then comes smoke and peat, but light. It's not an iodine-heavy taste, there's no billowing smoke.

**Finish:** At the end, the dominant fruit from the beginning finishes things off sweetly. I liked the second half more than the first :)

**Update, an hour later:** That bitter fruit taste I mentioned actually got better the more I drank :)

## Verdict

I think it's more of a cold weather whisky. Personally, it was a different experience for me, so I can't say whether it's a favourite or not :)

On weekdays I don't go past one double when I open it 😄

Sláinte! 🥃
