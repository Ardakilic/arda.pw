I picked this up at the duty free at Sabiha Gökçen Airport on my way back from a trip in September. It came to 33 € for a litre. It's Irish, not Scotch.

The brand's [#3 Bourbon Cask](/posts/bushmills-3-bourbon-cask-tasting-notes/) wasn't bad at all, so I bought it partly trusting that, partly because I'd never tried it, and partly because of the good price.

Then I realised I'd never written anything about it :) So I opened the Black Bush again tonight and scribbled down a few spontaneous notes while sipping.

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Whisky** | Bushmills Black Bush 80/20 PX Sherry Cask Reserve |
| **Type** | Irish Whiskey: 80% single malt finished in Pedro Ximénez sherry casks, 20% small batch grain |
| **ABV** | 40% |
| **Size** | 1 L (travel retail exclusive) |
| **Price paid** | 33 € (Sabiha Gökçen Airport duty free, September 2025) |

## Tasting Notes

**Nose:** Straight up, loads of grape and lemon. I liked it.

**Palate:** There are two tastes as you sip. When it first arrives it's quite milky, with vanilla?? But what follows is sharper: towards the end it leaves black tea, sesame? and cinnamon, a bitter but lovely spice, close to black pepper.

**Finish:** At the very end it leaves a fruity taste on the palate.

## Verdict

I liked it a lot. If you're someone who loves sherry cask whiskies, definitely try it. I'd had their Triple Cask before and it was great too.

I don't have much of an Irish whiskey background, but this brand I like is usually well priced, so I buy it whenever I see it :)
