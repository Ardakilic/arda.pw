I opened the Ardbeg For Discussion 8 Years.

This is the last of the three samples a friend brought me from a whisky tasting, after [Compass Box The Numinous Cask](/posts/compass-box-numinous-cask-near-celestial-tasting-notes/) and [MackMyra Intelligens AI:01](/posts/mackmyra-intelligens-ai01-tasting-notes/).

It's a strong drink, I'd say. Its taste and nose are very complex, but I liked it. Heavily smoky, with a relatively high ABV at 50.8%.

## At a Glance

| **Format** | Sample vial, from a friend |

## Tasting Notes

**Nose:** Smoky, of course: loads of smoke, with spices next to it like black pepper and cinnamon, and a bit of dried fruit (apricot and raisin).

**Palate:** The smoke is there at every point. As for the rest: it opens minty! First a hit of mint, then a heavy, fruity, lovely aroma that slides from green apple towards lemon.

**Finish:** It leaves mint / menthol in the throat, and a slightly sweet, dried-fruit-like taste. The longer you wait and the more the mint fades, the very, very end turns into something like honey.

## Verdict

Complex and strong, definitely not for everyone, but I liked it.
