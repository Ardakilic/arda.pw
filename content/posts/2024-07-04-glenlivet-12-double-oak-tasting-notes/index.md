Let me present tonight's companion ☺️ It's my first time tasting this one. On 30 June, the last day before the price hike, I rushed into Macrocenter and bought it.

I was actually after the Founder's Reserve, since a friend had praised it, but it was out of stock. People had also said the 12 is a decent entry point for those getting into single malts, so I took this one instead.

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Whisky** | The Glenlivet 12 Year Old Double Oak |
| **Type** | Single Malt Scotch Whisky |
| **Region** | Speyside |
| **Casks** | European oak & American oak |
| **ABV** | 40% |
| **Size** | 70 cl |
| **Where** | Macrocenter, June 2024 |

## Tasting Notes

**Nose:** Honestly, a whole lot of green apple. Right next to it, lemon and other citrus, and if I push it, some slightly oilier, nutty notes underneath, like vanilla?

**Palate:** It starts with citrus, lemon and kumquat, then finishes lower down with vanilla, even caramel(?). That's probably from the bourbon casks.

**Finish:** It leaves a light and pleasant taste in the throat.

## Verdict

Honestly, I think it's a summer drink. It's not smoky like I prefer, but it's the kind of thing you drink in summer. I liked it.

Sláinte! 🥃
