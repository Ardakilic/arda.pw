I picked this up at the end of August during a big spirits sale at Carrefour. The shelves were wiped out on the first day, so I went back when they brought out the second batch from the warehouse and grabbed this together with a [Ballantine's 7](/posts/ballantines-7-american-barrel-tasting-notes/).

I opened the Teeling just now. Let me try to put a quick review together.

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Whisky** | Teeling Single Malt |
| **Type** | Single Malt Irish Whiskey, non-chill filtered |
| **Region** | Dublin, Ireland |
| **ABV** | 46% |
| **Size** | 70 cl |
| **Where** | Carrefour sale, August 2026 |

## Tasting Notes

**Nose:** Loads of apple and pear, with honey deep down. Fruit-forward.

**Palate:** There's spice, oddly enough. I liked it.

**Finish:** As the fruit fades, it carries on with the spices, and then leaves something that flows like cream.. Not whipped cream, something thicker. I can't name it. It's not caramel either: somewhere between whipped cream and caramel, a light, creamy, soft something right at the very end.

## Verdict

I loved the aftertaste.

Sláinte! 🥃
