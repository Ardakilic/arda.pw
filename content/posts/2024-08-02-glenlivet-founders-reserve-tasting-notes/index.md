I bought this last week, The Glenlivet Founder's Reserve, and just opened it. A friend had recommended it, his notes were similar to mine, and when he praised it the way he did, I went and bought one.

Here are my humble notes.

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Whisky** | The Glenlivet Founder's Reserve (American Oak Selection) |
| **Type** | Single Malt Scotch Whisky |
| **Region** | Speyside |
| **Casks** | First-fill American oak |
| **ABV** | 40% |

## Tasting Notes

**Nose:** Peat and fresh fruit, with a light caramel and hazelnut-like smell underneath.

**Palate:** Very light and enjoyable to drink. A touch harsher than the nose suggests, but still pleasant, with flavours like sour fruit.

**Smoke:** None. From the nose I thought it was peated, but it isn't. It's matured in American oak casks.

## Verdict

A light, pleasant summer whisky. Even though I said the taste is a bit harsher than the nose suggests, it's still very light. I liked it a lot.

A couple of months later, my opinion only got better: Founder's Reserve is a really good value-for-money whisky and I'm very happy with it. Together with Teacher's and Monkey Shoulder, it's one of my everyday pours that won't burn a hole in your pocket.

Sláinte! 🥃
