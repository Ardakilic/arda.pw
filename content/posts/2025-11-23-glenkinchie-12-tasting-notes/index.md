My new bottle: Glenkinchie 12.

I bought it on my last trip together with a BenRiach The Twelve, both on recommendation. Both were good value; the two together came to 3300 TL.

I just opened it and poured a single into my Glencairn glass. Here are my notes while I slowly sip :)

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Whisky** | Glenkinchie 12 Year Old ("The Edinburgh Malt") |
| **Type** | Single Malt Scotch Whisky |
| **Region** | Lowlands |
| **ABV** | 43% |
| **Size** | 70 cl |
| **Price paid** | 3300 TL together with BenRiach The Twelve (October 2025) |

## Tasting Notes

**Nose:** Lemon, fresh flowers and fresh-cut grass. That fresh, green, herbal smell is front and centre. I even get a tiny bit of vanilla.

**Palate:** In line with the nose: lemon, lime and a slightly sour orange. Towards the end it gets drier on the palate: vanilla, almond, and every now and then, right at the very end, I even got a hint of peanut.

**Finish:** Those last notes take the lead in the aftertaste. The spicy side becomes more dominant, with a light almond and sweetness and just a little sourness. I loved that combo.

## Verdict

I love smoky whisky, but I liked this one. I recommend it :)

Sláinte! 🥃
