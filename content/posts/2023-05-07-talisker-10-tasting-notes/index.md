I'd bought a bottle of Talisker 10 a while back, and I finally opened it tonight 😋

I'm not a gourmet. These are just my honest notes on what I tasted.

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Whisky** | Talisker 10 Year Old |
| **Type** | Single Malt Scotch Whisky |
| **Region** | Isle of Skye |
| **ABV** | 45.8% |
| **Price paid** | ~50 € (duty free) |

## Tasting Notes

**Nose:** The moment I pulled the cork, smoke came pouring out from under it.

I poured it into the glass and let it rest 3–4 minutes so the alcohol could settle.

**Palate:** From the very first sip it lives up to its "Made by the Sea" label. Loads of salted caramel, to the point where it almost burns your throat :) The first tone is smoke, of course. Then the saltiness made me think of seafood: smoked fish, maybe squid, even samphire.

**Finish:** As you keep sipping, the lower tones bring spices I can't quite name, dried apricot, and right at the very bottom I'd even say vanilla.

## Pairing

I made a mistake opening salty nuts with it. Go straight for **dark chocolate and dried fruit** with this one.

## Verdict

I recommend it. Talisker 10 has since become my standard answer to every whisky question: *"Talisker Dark Storm, and if you can't find it, the 10."*

For reference, a few months later the 70 cl bottle was 1450 TL at Migros, so the duty free price was quite a good deal.

Sláinte! 🥃
