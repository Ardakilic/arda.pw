I bought this as more of a default, everyday whisky for myself. I'd never tried this version before. It's aged for 7 years exclusively in American oak (ex-bourbon) barrels, and the concept sounded interesting to me. The bottle also promised honey, cookie and fruit notes. I didn't want Jameson, so I went for this one 😅

I opened it tonight.

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Whisky** | Ballantine's 7 Year Old American Barrel |
| **Type** | Blended Scotch Whisky |
| **ABV** | 40% |
| **Size** | 70 cl |
| **Price paid** | ~750 TL / 70 cl (about 1100 TL per litre at Carrefour & Macrocenter, June 2024) |

## Tasting Notes

**Nose:** For someone who loves peaty/smoky whisky, the nose felt weak to me. It's light, but in line with its character. Citrus, and vanilla, maybe? It has a fresh smell. It could have been a bit more intense.

**Palate:** First come citrus fruits (especially lemon?), then vanilla, and towards the end even something like cinnamon?

**Finish:** As the fruit and cinnamon fade, it carries on with something like black pepper. That's probably the effect of the American bourbon barrels.

## Verdict

I liked it. I'd call it a spring/autumn whisky, but I'd drink it in summer too. I bought it at a good time :)

I'm not a gourmet. I just tried to write what I felt, I hope it helps :)
