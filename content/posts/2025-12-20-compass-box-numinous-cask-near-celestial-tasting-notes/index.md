A friend brought me three samples from a whisky tasting. Let's see, I'm starting with one of them tonight 🤓

The three samples were this one, [MackMyra Intelligens AI:01](/posts/mackmyra-intelligens-ai01-tasting-notes/) and [Ardbeg For Discussion 8 Years](/posts/ardbeg-for-discussion-8-tasting-notes/). I'm starting with **Compass Box – The Numinous Cask – Near Celestial**.

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Whisky** | Compass Box The Numinous Cask – Near Celestial |
| **Type** | Scotch Whisky |
| **ABV** | 50.3% |
| **Format** | Sample vial, from a friend |

## Tasting Notes

**Nose:** Smells of peat like crazy, and you can tell it's high in alcohol (50.3%). This might be the most peat-smelling whisky I've ever had. Behind that, there's a very faint spice too.

**Palate:** For the first time ever, I got fresh orange in a whisky 🙂 And it's really nice. The beginning is very much like fresh orange. As that citrus carries on with peat and grass, towards the end it gives way to a lot of spice. It goes back and forth between mint? cinnamon? black pepper?

**Finish:** Once the alcohol fades, it leaves a taste like almond cream on the palate.

## Verdict

I liked it.

Sláinte! 🥃
