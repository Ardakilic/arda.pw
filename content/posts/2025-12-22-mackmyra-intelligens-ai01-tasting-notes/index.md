I opened the MackMyra.

This is the second of three samples a friend brought me from a whisky tasting (the others being [Compass Box The Numinous Cask](/posts/compass-box-numinous-cask-near-celestial-tasting-notes/) and [Ardbeg For Discussion 8 Years](/posts/ardbeg-for-discussion-8-tasting-notes/)).

The fun part for someone like me who writes software for a living: the master blender on this one is an AI. The formulation was generated by artificial intelligence, supervised by a human master blender :)

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Whisky** | MackMyra Intelligens AI:01 |
| **Type** | Swedish Single Malt Whisky |
| **Master blender** | Artificial Intelligence, supervised by Angela D'Orazio |
| **ABV** | 46.1% |
| **Format** | Sample vial, from a friend |

## Tasting Notes

**Nose:** Floral, honeyed, plummy and citrusy. A light, lovely nose.

**Palate:** Apple and honeyed fig, plus a little something else sweet I can't quite pin down 🙂 A sweetness somewhere between vanilla and clotted cream..

**Finish:** The citrus tones… like lemon, take over, with a very faint vanilla gliding underneath.

## Verdict

Perfect for sipping right on the beach. I liked it :)

Sláinte! 🥃
