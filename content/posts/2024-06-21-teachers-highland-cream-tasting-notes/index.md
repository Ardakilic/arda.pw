I only meant to buy Monkey Shoulder's Smokey Monkey that day. While browsing, I spotted Laphroaig 10 right next to it, a whisky I've been very curious about for its smoky character. It was 2.5k TL for 70 cl, a bit pricey, but I thought *why not, let me try it*. I reached for the box… and it was empty. No bottle behind it either. Someone had apparently taken just the bottle 😂

Then I saw Teacher's on the other side. A friend had recommended it, saying it would suit my taste, so I grabbed it straight away 😅

I just poured it into my glass and scribbled some notes.

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Whisky** | Teacher's Highland Cream |
| **Type** | Blended Scotch Whisky, "High in Peated Malt" |
| **ABV** | 40% |
| **Size** | 70 cl |
| **Price paid** | 775 TL (Carrefour, June 2024) |

## Tasting Notes

**Nose:** It reminded me a lot of green apple, a bit of mint and vanilla. There's a fresh, herbal smell too, which must be the phenolic aromas coming from the peat used in the kilning. Very deep down there's a little honey and apricot(?). On top of all that, there's a salty smell that keeps popping up to say hello.

**Palate:** Quite salty, with a thick, almost creamy texture. Caramel, maybe? I couldn't find the characters from the nose on the palate. The smoke and its friends seem to overpower those fresh aromas (well, it's a smoky whisky after all).

**Finish:** Towards the end, besides the smoke and salt, it leaves a hint of mint(?) and black pepper. It could be a little bolder, but at this price I'm not complaining.

## Pairing

I opened some dark chocolate alongside it.

## Verdict

Not bad at all for the price, and considering it's smoky, it's very good value. For now I'd pick it over Black Label, let me put it that way, eheh.

A few months later, it's still in my rotation: Teacher's, Monkey Shoulder (both the original and the Smokey one) and Glenlivet Founder's Reserve are all whiskies you can sip every day without burning a hole in your pocket.

A fun bit of trivia I learned later: Teacher's gets its malt from the Ardmore distillery.

Sláinte! 🥃
